Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Magan Solutions ("we", "us", "our") collects when you use ResuMagan (the "Service"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the Service you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account information. Your name and email address, and how you sign in (email and password, or Google). Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we cannot see your password.

Resume content. Everything you enter or import: name, job title, email, phone number, location, website, profile photo, professional summary, work experience, education, skills, projects, certifications, awards, languages, interests and custom sections. When you upload a PDF or DOCX file, we read it to extract this content. We do not keep the original file.

Design choices. The template, fonts, colours and style you pick for each resume.

AI activity. The questions you ask the AI Assistant and its answers (your chat history), your Summary Generator and ATS actions shown in "Recent AI Actions", and the text you send to AI features.

Profile photo. If you add one, a small version of it is stored with your profile.

Notifications and technical data. A Firebase Cloud Messaging device token and your notification choice, so we can send you messages. Google services also process technical data such as IP address, device model, operating system, app version and app-integrity signals (Play Integrity and App Check) to keep the Service secure.

On your device only. Settings, a daily AI-usage counter, saved template previews and temporary PDF files. These stay on your phone.

Advertising and analytics data. The Service shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to deliver, measure and (where the law allows and with your consent) personalise ads. We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Service is used, such as screens viewed, errors and ad performance, and Firebase Remote Config to adjust app settings. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before ads that use personal data are shown, and you can change your choice at any time under Profile > Ad privacy settings.

We do not collect your precise location or your contacts.

2. How we use information

  • to provide the Service: save your resumes, show templates, create PDFs and sync your data to your account;
  • to power AI features and the on-device ATS check;
  • to send notifications and announcements you have allowed;
  • to keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, fix problems and improve it;
  • to show and measure ads, and to understand how the app is used;
  • to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising partners receive identifiers and ad-interaction data as described in this policy.

3. AI features and Google Gemini

When you use the Summary Generator, the AI Assistant, or upload a resume, the relevant text (and, for a PDF upload, the content of the file) is sent to Google's Gemini service through Firebase AI Logic to produce a result. The ATS check runs on your device and sends nothing. Google processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy (see policies.google.com/privacy). Please do not include sensitive information in AI requests. We may limit how many AI requests you can make each day.

4. Who we share information with

  • Service providers. We use Google Firebase and related Google services (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, App Check, AI Logic and Google Sign-In) to run the Service. They process data for us under their own terms.
  • Legal reasons. If the law requires it, or to protect rights, safety and security.
  • Advertising partners. Google AdMob and its partners receive your advertising ID and technical and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • Business changes. If we merge with or are acquired by another company, your information may move to it, and we will tell you.
  • When you choose. If you download or share a PDF with your phone's share menu, whoever receives it gets your resume.

Your resumes are private to your account, and we do not publish them.

5. Where information is stored

Our providers may store and process data on servers in different countries. By using the Service you understand that your information may be processed outside your own country, under the safeguards our providers apply.

6. How long we keep information, and deleting it

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete a single resume, clear your AI chat history and remove your profile photo at any time in the app. Profile > Delete Account permanently deletes your account and the data we hold in it: resumes, AI and chat history, profile and device tokens. Copies may remain in backups or logs for a short time before they are overwritten, and we may keep limited information where the law requires it. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

7. Your choices and rights

  • View and edit your information in the app.
  • Download your resumes as PDF files.
  • Turn notifications off in Profile or in your phone's settings.
  • Delete your content or your whole account, as described above.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask for access to, correction, deletion or a copy of your data, to object to or limit some processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Contact us and we will help.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and our database rules allow each signed-in user to reach only their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please protect your password and your device.

9. Children

The Service is not meant for people under 16, or under the age of digital consent in their country. We do not knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is important, we will tell you in the app or by another suitable method, and we will update the date at the top.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy? Contact magansolution@gmail.com.
