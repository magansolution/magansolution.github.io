Account & data deletion

Delete your ResuMagan account and data

This page explains how to delete your account and the data stored with it for the ResuMagan Android app (package com.magansolutions.resumagan), published by Magan Solutions.

Fastest · instant

Option 1: Delete inside the app

You can delete your account yourself in the ResuMagan app. Everything is removed immediately.

  1. Open the ResuMagan app and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the warning and tap Delete to confirm.
  5. Confirm it is you: enter your password (email accounts), or choose your Google account (Google sign-in accounts).
  6. Wait for the message “Your account was deleted”. You are then signed out.
This cannot be undone. Download any resumes you want to keep as PDF files before you delete your account.
No app access needed

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you uninstalled the app, cannot sign in, or cannot open the app, you can ask us to delete your account and data.

  1. Send an email to magansolution@gmail.com from the email address you used to register, with the subject “Delete my ResuMagan account”.
    Email a deletion request
  2. We may reply to confirm that the request really comes from you. Replying from the registered address is enough.
  3. We delete your account and data and send you a confirmation. We complete requests within 30 days, usually much sooner.
Please do not send passwords, ID numbers or other sensitive details in your request. The email address is all we need.

What we delete

When your account is deleted, we permanently delete the following from our systems:

DataWhat it includesResult
Account Your email address, name, and your sign-in details (email & password sign-in, or the link to your Google sign-in). Deleted
Resumes Everything in your saved resumes and drafts: personal details, profile photo, summary, work experience, education, skills, projects, certifications, awards, languages, interests, custom sections, and the template and design you chose. Deleted
AI data Your AI Assistant chat history, your “Recent AI actions” (such as generated summaries and ATS checks) and the text saved with them. Deleted
Profile Your profile picture and profile settings stored with your account. Deleted
Notifications The notification tokens of your devices that we saved under your account. Deleted

What we may keep, and for how long

DataWhy and how long
Backups and system logs Copies of deleted data can remain in secure backups or technical logs for a short time until they are overwritten. This is up to 30 days. We do not use them for any other purpose.
Usage and ad data held by Google The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Google AdMob. Analytics data is used in aggregated or pseudonymous form and is kept according to Google’s own retention settings. It is no longer linked to a ResuMagan account once the account is deleted. Ad-related data is controlled by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your phone’s settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads or Settings > Google > Ads).
Legal or security records We keep the minimum information we are required by law to keep, or need to prevent fraud and abuse (for example, a record that a deletion request was made). If we do keep something, we keep it only for as long as the law or the purpose requires.
Files you already downloaded or shared PDF resumes you saved to your phone or sent to other people are outside our control. Please delete them yourself.

Delete only some of your data

You can remove specific data and keep your account. In the app:

  • A resume: open the resume’s menu (⋮) and tap Delete.
  • AI chat history: AI Assistant > open the chat > history icon > Clear.
  • Profile photo: Profile > tap your photo > Remove photo.
  • Notifications: Profile > turn off Notifications.

You can also email magansolution@gmail.com to ask us to delete specific data.

Questions?

Contact Magan Solutions at magansolution@gmail.com. For how we handle data in general, see our Privacy Policy in the ResuMagan app (Profile > Privacy Policy).
